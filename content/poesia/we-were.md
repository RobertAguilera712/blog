Title: What We Were
Date: 2026-09-03
Category: Poesía
Tags: Off Topic, Poesía, Personal

> We were
>
> What were we?
>
> You were all for me,
>
> I was none for you.

<br/>

> We were
>
> Together for a while
>
> Where are we now?
>
> I'm losing my life,
>
> You're living wild.
>

<br/>

> We were
>
> Whatever we were
>
> It's not what we are
>
> nor what we'll be.
